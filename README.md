## Building dependable systems at scale.

I'm **Amrita Singh**, a Staff Software Engineer with 14+ years of experience designing, building and evolving enterprise software.

At Walmart Global Tech, I work on marketplace notification platforms serving sellers, suppliers and partners. My experience spans backend engineering, distributed systems, event streaming, data migrations, platform security and production reliability. I work across teams to turn system-level design decisions into dependable production outcomes.

More recently, I've also worked on practical AI integrations, including MCP-based webhook operations and service/API integration for a RAG capability used by Walmart's seller-facing AI assistant, Marty.

> **My engineering lens:** thoughtful architecture, clear trade-offs, operational ownership and solutions that work in production.
